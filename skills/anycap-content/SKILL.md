# AnyCap Content

Choose strategy or drafting. Both routes use a claim ledger and live evidence,
but SEO decides which page should exist while drafting writes an approved page.

Do not load every reference. Route missing multi-source evidence to
`anycap-research`; use `anycap-access` only for CLI setup or low-level failures.

## Shared workflow

1. Freeze `reader | intent | conversion/job | supplied evidence | supported
   claims | unknowns | requested output`.
2. For SEO, inspect the live site and SERP before selecting page type. Separate
   rules, practitioner heuristics, and assumptions. Do not promise rankings or
   scale pSEO before useful sample pages prove quality and indexation.
3. For drafting, write answer-first from the approved evidence ledger. Remove
   warm-up, duplicated explanation, vague superlatives, and unsupported claims.
4. Verify every capability, benchmark, comparison, limitation, and CTA against
   the source that actually supports it.

Explanatory visuals begin at `image-gen`; exact systems at
`image-gen-system`; exact charts at `chart-gen`; explicit/delegated AnyCap media
routes to `anycap-media`. Generated media is not product evidence.

## Output and write boundary

Return the SEO plan/audit or content draft plus concise unsupported/uncertain
claim notes. Local output is the default. Code edits, Drive/Page delivery,
publication, or remote updates require explicit operation and target
authorization; strategy or drafting approval does not authorize them.
