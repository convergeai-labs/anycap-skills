# Social and campaign video production

Use this reference when the campaign depends on a public reference set, a UGC
mode, a repair claim, or one uninterrupted shot. It strengthens campaign
storytelling without turning this Skill into a generic video-research or editing
owner.

## Evidence before imitation

Keep a dated source registry with URLs, saved media metadata, hashes when local
copies are retained, and one case note per creative asset. Cross-posts are one
creative with several distribution records. For every production claim use:

- `observed`: visible frames, captions, public metadata, or recorded prompt;
- `inferred`: a production explanation consistent with the visible result;
- `speculative`: an unverified reconstruction or model/prompt hypothesis.

Prefer patterns repeated across at least three independent creatives. A failed
public search means `not publicly verified`, not that the asset does not exist.

## Select exactly one primary mode

| Mode | Acceptance test |
| --- | --- |
| launch / brand film | promise, visible mechanism, proof, and CTA form one campaign story |
| UGC generation | the result shows a meaningful physical action every 1.5–4 seconds |
| UGC repair | every named defect maps to a replacement beat and final check |
| transformation | the new subject/style preserves enough continuous motion to compare |
| one-take promo | no editorial cut, camera teleport, location morph, or continuity reset |

A localized edit to an existing clip belongs to `anycap-media`. A
software architecture, request, state, incident, release, or AI Coding explainer
starts at `video-gen`.

## Generation gate

Freeze the brief, continuity anchors, storyboard, start/end states, contact
points, and per-beat physical action before paid generation. Inspect the live
schema only for the selected model and mode. Keep benchmark challengers outside
the production queue until they have the same-input acceptance evidence.

Treat upload, TLS, DNS, and artifact-download failures as transport evidence:
retry the same request only when supported and bounded, then stop. Do not switch
to another paid model unless the failure is classified as model/provider-related
and the fallback was approved.

## Final QA

Keep model-generated plates free of exact captions, logos, URLs, and UI proof;
composite those deterministically. Save prompt/provenance sidecars, a contact
sheet, technical metadata, motion review, `video-read`, and audio review when
speech or a deliberate mix exists. The visible result—not narration about an
off-screen action—must prove the selected mode's acceptance test.
